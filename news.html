NEWS 01

UC Berkeley professor raises concerns about calculus students' preparation

A mathematics professor argued that some students entering calculus are arriving with substantial gaps in foundational mathematics. The discussion has focused on whether university-level calculus courses are increasingly having to address prerequisite skills rather than concentrating exclusively on calculus itself.

NEWS 02

12 colleges are redesigning gateway calculus courses

A major higher-education development from July is an initiative involving 12 colleges that are redesigning introductory/gateway mathematics courses to help more students successfully progress through Calculus I and II. The motivation is that introductory calculus can become a bottleneck for students pursuing STEM degrees.

NEWS 03

Carnegie Mellon is using AI to improve Calculus

Carnegie Mellon University launched Learnvia, a nonprofit learning initiative developed with the Gates Foundation. Its initial work includes Calculus I, with AI-enhanced courseware intended to help students develop mathematics skills and improve success in high-enrollment college courses.

NEWS 04

New Calculus support from McGraw Hill

McGraw Hill recently expanded its AI-powered ALEKS platform with ALEKS for Calculus II, extending its adaptive mathematics system into a more advanced university-level calculus course. The company says ALEKS has been used by more than 65 million students across K–12 and higher education
