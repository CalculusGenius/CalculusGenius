How to use the universe

Explore without a finish line.

Try a familiar function, then mutate it. Increase the order. Move the expansion point. Change the initial condition. Reverse an interval. Approach a singularity. Compare two experiments. Generate another challenge.

There is no fixed sequence and no “last experiment”. The available parameter combinations create an open-ended space of investigations.
