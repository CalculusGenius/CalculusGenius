04 · CHAOS

Order can
break itself.

The logistic map is a one-line recurrence. Change one parameter and long-term behaviour passes through fixed points, cycles and chaos.

BIFURCATION

A tiny rule. Wild behaviour.

There is no random generator here. The apparent disorder comes from deterministic iteration and sensitivity to the parameter.

TRY: shift the window and inspect the boundary between periodic behaviour and chaos.
