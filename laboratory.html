02 · DYNAMICAL SYSTEM

Three equations.
Infinite weather.

The Lorenz equations produce deterministic trajectories with sensitive dependence on initial conditions. Change ρ and watch the phase portrait reorganise itself.

LIVE COMPUTATION

The butterfly
is a phase space.

dx/dt = σ(y − x)
dy/dt = x(ρ − z) − y
dz/dt = xy − βz

Thousands of numerical integration steps are generated locally. There is no video, no prerecorded animation and no scroll-driven render loop.
