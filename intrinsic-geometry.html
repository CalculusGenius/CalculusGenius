Intrinsic Geometry and the Gauss Theorema Egregium

Overview

Intrinsic geometry concerns measurements made within a surface. Gauss's theorem shows that Gaussian curvature is determined by the metric.

This page is a concise introduction to the topic. Detailed definitions, theorems, examples, and exercises can be developed here as the CALCULUS topic library expands.
