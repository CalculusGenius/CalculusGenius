CALCULUS

Calculus is the most glorified and intriguing branch of mathematics. It is special because it can solve problems from enormous ranges, even by using approximation. It connects accuracy and approximation and shows their transition

Purpose of the Project

The Main purpose of this project is to showcase that mathematics does not care on age. I am specifically emphasizing on the fact that even eighth graders can study and learn Differential Geometry, and if they wish to, they can even begin manifolds. Mathematics does not see your age. Its ourselves, who have set age restrictions, even for studies
